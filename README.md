# my COMP6445 study notes

these are notes i made prepping for the final and as the term went by!

i use notion to organise my uni notes but i decided to turn it into a webapp as a new way to motivate myself to study (#burnout) + helpful to look back in the future and incase it helps others interested in digi forensics as well

## structure
- one file per week in `data/` (`week1.js`, `week2.js`, …).
- each week has four sections:
  - **Professional**: professional lectures (ethics, law, cases, method).
  - **Practice**: technical lectures.
  - **Tutorial**: classes
  - **Challenges**: the *skills* from the wargames (never the challenges themselves).
- each section has **learn** (a short explanation) and **test** (flashcards).

## Content
this repo and site is public, so:
- everything is in my own words from the course content. no copied slides, lecture text, recordings or transcripts 
to comply w policy.
- no assignment reports, sample exam papers or wargame/challenge text, files or flags are in this either
- public court cases and public tools are described from general knowledge.

## private section
tutorial and Challenges show a general, public version, BUT there is a locked box with my full
write ups and the flags (the questions themselves can't be published under course policy).
The **private** tab is for my own material: challenge text, my working, reports and raw brain thinking.
